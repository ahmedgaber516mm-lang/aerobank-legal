Aero Bank

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Aero Bank collects, uses and stores information when you interact with our Discord bot.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Aero Bank may collect and store:

  • Discord User IDs.
  • Discord Server IDs.
  • Virtual account balances and gold.
  • Transaction records.
  • Salary, loan and repayment information.
  • Economy settings and account-related data.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Operate and maintain user accounts.
  • Process transfers and transactions.
  • Manage salaries, loans and repayments.
  • Provide leaderboards and economy features.
  • Prevent abuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Maintain and improve the service.

4. Data Storage and Security

Relevant information is stored in our database to operate Aero Bank. Data may also be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information. However, no storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Authorized Aero Bank administrators may access relevant data when necessary for support, security or maintenance.

Discord and hosting providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as necessary to operate the service and maintain its economy system.

Users may contact the Aero Bank support team to ask about their stored data or request deletion. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period where technically necessary.

7. Third-Party Services

Aero Bank operates through Discord and may rely on external hosting services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the Aero Bank support team through our official Discord support server.
